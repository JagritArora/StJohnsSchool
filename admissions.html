    How to apply

    Five steps, from first question to first day.

    1. EnquireFill the short form on this page. The office calls you back, usually the same working day.
    2. VisitSee the classrooms, grounds and stage, and meet the admissions team.
    3. RegisterRegister with a copy of the date of birth certificate and residence proof. Enter the name and date of birth exactly right: they cannot be changed later.
    4. DocumentsBring the originals for verification along with photocopies.
    5. ConfirmationAdmission is on merit. Selected Nursery names go up on the notice board at reception, and on this website.

    Age criteria

    Questions parents ask

    Not answered here?

    Call the office on 0129 224 2097 or 0129 224 8895, or send an enquiry and they will call you.

    When does the admission process begin?
    Fill the enquiry form at any time. The office will tell you the registration dates for your child's class as soon as they are announced.
    How are Nursery seats decided?
    Admission is on merit, as per the school's admission criteria. The office explains the criteria for your child's class when you enquire.
    Can my child join in a class other than Nursery or XI?
    Sometimes. Seats in other classes open up when families move. Choose "Other class" in the enquiry form and the office will tell you whether a seat is available.
    How will I know if my child has been selected?
    Selected Nursery names are put up on the notice board at reception. With the new website, families who enquire online also get an SMS and can check here.
    What happens if my child does not pass Class X after provisional admission to XI?
    The provisional admission is cancelled automatically. It becomes regular once the CBSE Class X result and documents are submitted.
    Does the school run buses?
    Yes. Route details are shared at admission, and parents can follow the bus live in the parent app. See school transport.
    Can the name or date of birth be corrected after the form is submitted?
    No. Fill in the child's name and date of birth exactly as on the birth certificate, and complete every field. No change is allowed afterwards.
    What are the school timings?
    Nursery and KG: Monday to Friday, 8:35 AM to 12:55 PM in summer and 9:00 AM to 1:20 PM in winter. Classes I to XII: Monday to Saturday, 7:35 AM to 1:45 PM in summer and 8:05 AM to 2:15 PM in winter.
    Does submitting the form guarantee admission?
    No. Submitting a form does not guarantee a seat. Provisional and regular admission are at the school's discretion.
