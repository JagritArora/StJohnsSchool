Activities

Every child takes part in something. Most take part in several.

Choir and music

From devotional songs with live tabla and sitar to the full Annual Day choir, music is a big part of school life, all the way to carols at Christmas.

The school band

Red berets, tartan waistcoats and drums. The band plays at assemblies, leads the march-past on Independence and Republic Day, and welcomes guests on prize days.

Dance from every state

Garba from Gujarat, folk dances from the North-East, and more every year. Our celebrations of India's many cultures are how national integration is taught here.

Sport and Fit India

Athletics, team games and Sports Day, and the Fit India movement for everyday fitness. Trophies won are shown off at the prize day.

Houses

Every student from Class I to XII belongs to a house named after a great Indian, led by a junior and a senior House Captain. Houses compete all year in sport, dramatics, declamation, quiz, music and art, and each hosts the morning assembly for a fortnight. House names and colours: to be added.

Student Council

Chosen each year through a democratic process with peers, teachers and candidates. The council carries students' ideas to the teachers and the Principal, plans events and runs service projects.

Morning assembly

A prayer and the pledge, then a thought for the day, a song, dance or skit by the house on duty, and the news headlines. Special assemblies mark days like Republic Day, Teachers' Day, Onam, Eid, Diwali and Christmas.
