Safety and wellbeing

Parents should never have to wonder whether their child is safe at school. These are the arrangements the website will publish once the school confirms them.

  • CCTV at gates, corridors and common areas
  • Visitor passes and a register at the main gate
  • Children handed over only to registered guardians
  • Trained staff for first aid and fire drills
  • A counsellor and a committee for the protection of children, as CBSE requires
  • Fire, building and water safety certificates, published on the disclosure page
  • Absence alerts to parents within minutes of roll call, through the parent app

School transport
