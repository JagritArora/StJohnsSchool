Fees and payment

Fees follow CBSE norms and are paid in instalments. The fee structure for the session is published in Downloads.

  1. See what's dueThe parent app shows each instalment, its due date and what's been paid.
  2. Pay by UPIGPay, PhonePe, Paytm or any bank app, straight from the app.
  3. Get the receiptA receipt arrives at once, on WhatsApp and email, and stays in the app.

Instalment dates and amounts: to be added by the accounts office.
