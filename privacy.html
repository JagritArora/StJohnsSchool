Children's data

  • A parent or guardian gives consent for their child's data at admission, and can withdraw it by writing to the school.
  • Each parent sees only their own child. Teachers see only the classes they teach.
  • No advertising, no tracking of children across other websites, and no selling or sharing of data for marketing.
  • Data stays with the school and the service providers that run the app for it, under written agreements, and is stored in India.
  • Records are kept only as long as the school, CBSE or the law requires, then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask the school to show you the data it holds about you or your child, to correct anything that is wrong, and to delete what it no longer needs. You can also name someone to act for you if you are unable to.

If you are not satisfied with the school's answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
